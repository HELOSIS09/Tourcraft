How TourCraft reads your hotel budget

Accommodation should support the trip, not consume it. These tiers are designed to make it easier to decide how much of your total travel budget to allocate to a stay.

Smart Budget ₹1k–₹3.5k Clean private rooms, hostels, guesthouses and practical locations. Best when experiences matter more than luxury.
Comfort ₹3.5k–₹8k Comfortable 3-star and boutique stays with better rooms, facilities and a little more breathing room.
Premium ₹8k+ Upscale hotels, resorts and heritage properties for travellers who want the stay itself to be part of the experience.
